# Script and storyboard

Use the client's verified language and tone. Separate spoken copy from production directions.

Produce:

1. A concise creative brief.
2. Narration with a clear hook, value, evidence, and call to action.
3. A time-coded storyboard with one row per shot.
4. An asset plan marking every shot as `real_footage`, `avatar`, `generated_illustration`, `licensed_stock`, or `graphic`.
5. On-screen text and pronunciation notes.
6. Claims or details requiring client confirmation.

For factual demonstrations such as vehicle condition, repairs, products, facilities, or before/after results, default to real footage. AI-generated visuals may illustrate a concept but must not serve as evidence.

Keep the script in `script_review` until a human approves the exact version.
