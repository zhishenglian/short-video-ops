# Topic and content planning

First obtain the validated output of `client-brand-context`.

For each proposed topic provide:

- audience problem or intent;
- opening hook;
- content pillar and funnel stage;
- factual evidence or real footage needed;
- primary call to action;
- target platform, duration, and format;
- hypothesis and success metric.

Balance educational, proof, objection-handling, offer, and brand-trust content. Do not treat an unverified trend as a fact. Do not reuse a competitor's wording or structure too closely.

Output a prioritized plan. Keep rejected or deferred ideas separate from approved production candidates.
